About me

Technical thinking with a creative eye.

My professional background is in software development, where I've spent over a year building real, production applications — monitoring dashboards, alerting systems and management platforms using React.js, Go, Laravel, MySQL and ClickHouse.

Day to day, that means building reusable UI components, integrating REST APIs, working with backend services, and debugging across the UI, API, backend and database layers to ship reliable features.

Alongside development, I take on freelance website projects. This lets me combine structured frontend development with visual design, layout, content and business-focused presentation for local businesses and personal brands.

I don't just want a website to exist. I want it to make the business look better, clearer and more trustworthy.
